Four safeguards built into the design

Leakage-safe by construction

Only information available at the prediction point enters the model — the end of semester one. Enforced by tests.

Sensitive attributes stay out

Never model inputs and never shown as reasons. They are used only to audit outcomes by group. Enforced by tests.

A human decides, every time

Acknowledgement is required before any action is recorded. Advisers can dismiss or override. Actions stay local and never feed back into the model.

A written non-use policy

No automated or adverse decision on admission, enrollment, aid, grades, discipline or housing. Set out in the project constitution.
